## 2. How Delta makes collaboration seamless

### 2.1 One record per thread

- **What the person sees.** A thread is the conversation and the files together, and "everyone sees messages, edits, comments, and agent activity as they happen, and everyone can steer the agent" ([Collaborate in a Thread](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread)). There is nothing to commit or push to show someone work in progress: "You can review and comment on anyone's work, agent or collaborator, in the middle of a turn … None of it involves a commit" ([Delta & Git](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/delta-and-git#what-delta-records-that-git-doesnt)).
- **Underneath.** DeltaDB "records thread messages, comments, Delta worktree changes, and file edits as deltas", and "each connected Delta client holds a copy of the database" ([Delta & Git](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/delta-and-git#what-is-deltadb)). It is "a collection of CRDTs, all stored locally in SQLite and linked together in a directed acyclic graph"; "changes are applied locally right away and synchronized when possible" ([engineering post](https://delta.dev/blog/fine-grained-version-control-for-agents#architecture)). A client subscribes to timelines over a WebSocket, catches up by walking the graph to a common ancestor, then sends each new delta, which is "saved by the server, and pushed to every other client subscribed to that timeline" ([§ timelines](https://delta.dev/blog/fine-grained-version-control-for-agents#timelines-and-synchronization)). "every thread gets synchronized to its own Cloudflare Durable Object", with one WebSocket per thread per client ([§ one Durable Object per thread](https://delta.dev/blog/fine-grained-version-control-for-agents#one-durable-object-per-thread)). The post's example models a thread as a transcript `Buffer`, a name `Register`, a `Worktree` and a `Map` of comments ([§ `#[derive(Replicate)]`](https://delta.dev/blog/fine-grained-version-control-for-agents#derivereplicate)); it is an illustration, not a published schema.

### 2.2 Files without push and pull

- **What the person sees.** Joining a thread on the desktop "creates isolated checkouts for any attached projects, even if you already have the repositories on your machine. You do not need to clone them first" ([Join a thread](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#join-a-thread)). Edits on any participant's machine show up in everyone else's checkout: "DeltaDB takes care of ensuring that all changes are synced between the two in real time" ([Delta Worktrees](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/worktrees#checkouts-a-delta-worktrees-files-on-a-machine)).
- **Underneath.** A worktree "starts at a commit, and each file is either pinned to its committed blob or carries the deltas written since. Creating a commit produces a delta too, which is what ties our DAG to Git's" ([engineering post](https://delta.dev/blog/fine-grained-version-control-for-agents#derivereplicate)). Delta imports tracked files and untracked files Git doesn't ignore; "commits stay in Git. Delta moves them between machines as needed, so a collaborator's machine can get your current commit without a round trip through origin" ([Delta & Git](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/delta-and-git#open-a-project)). A joiner's checkout is created "preferring an existing local checkout of the repository as the source and fetching from the same remote it uses" ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md)). Ignored files stay on their machine; `.agents/linked` symlinks listed ignored files, such as `.env`, into managed checkouts ([Delta Worktrees](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/worktrees#configuring-your-checkouts)).
- **Where threads work.** Isolated Delta worktree (the default), Existing Local Checkout, or Existing Git Worktree, "which lists linked Git worktrees already created with Git, an editor, or another agent". The choice locks at the first message, and threads sharing a checkout "are not isolated" ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md); [Delta Worktrees](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/worktrees#using-the-local-clone-with-delta)). Linked Git worktrees arrived in [0.18.0](https://delta.dev/docs/whats-in-the-latest#0.18.0) and became a possible default in 0.23.0.
- **Bringing work out.** In place, by pushing to a `local` remote that points at the person's own repository, through the source remote, or by copying from the checkout ([Delta & Git](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/delta-and-git#how-work-comes-back-out)).

### 2.3 Sharing and access

- **Levels.** "Only people invited", "Everyone at *organization name*", or "Anyone with the link" ("Any signed-in Delta user who has the link"). "Only the thread's owner can change who has access" ([Choose who can access a thread](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#choose-who-can-access-a-thread)). Email invitations go up to ten at a time, "expire after fourteen days", and the recipient "join[s] as an editor" ([Invite people by email](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#invite-people-by-email)). There is no viewer role: "Read and View permissions" is an Up Next roadmap item (section 4).
- **Organizations**, which the plugin calls teams, are made on Zed's dashboard or with `delta org create`; "Members can open every thread shared with the organization" ([CLI guide](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/get-started#manage-organizations)). A thread "is shared in the organization that was selected when it was created" ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md)).
- **Repositories.** "Sharing a thread grants access to its attached repositories. Delta worktree history access is repository-wide, so this also grants access to every Delta worktree history for each attached repository, including histories that are not attached to the shared thread" ([Data Storage](https://delta.dev/docs/privacy-and-security/data-storage#where-your-data-lives)). Access from GitHub's own repository permissions is In Progress (section 4).
- **Default.** New threads are private unless **Default Visibility** is set to the organization ([Collaborate in a Thread](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#choose-who-can-access-a-thread)). Storing a thread on the server "does not share it" ([Data Storage](https://delta.dev/docs/privacy-and-security/data-storage)).

### 2.4 Presence, following and links

- **Participant bar.** People in the thread show as avatars; review verdicts show "as badges on the reviewers' avatars". "Collaborators connected through a supported external tool such as Claude Code appear in the thread's participant bar with a badge, and their tooltip names the tool, while none of their Delta apps has the thread open" ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md)). Underneath, the CLI's watch emits a `presence` event listing users ([fixtures](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/testing/fixtures/cli/thread-watch.frames.jsonl#L11)), and a watch counts as presence only with `--participate` (3.5).
- **Following.** "Click their avatar and choose the **Follow** action"; following tracks the conversation, diffs, files, subagents and subthreads, and mirrors image zoom and pan ([Chat with collaborators](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#chat-with-collaborators)).
- **Links.** "Copy Link to Selection" in the conversation, a file, a diff or a comment, or "Copy Link to This Position"; "The link focuses that passage or position when opened. It does not grant access" ([Share a thread](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#share-a-thread)). A shared link "opens at your current position in the thread".

### 2.5 Drafts, sending, and whose turn it is

- **Drafts are shared.** "Each participant composes their own draft, attributed to them; you can edit someone else's draft." Drafts "are visible to everyone in the thread as they are typed", "sync to every participant, stay if you close and reopen the thread, and anyone can edit or delete them", so talking at the end of the thread "reads like a chat" ([Chat with collaborators](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#chat-with-collaborators)).
- **Simultaneous composers become one turn.** "Sending then submits every draft at the end of the thread, from every author, and the agent responds to all of them in one turn charged to the sender. There is no way to send a message to collaborators only." Pending comments go too, "including ones a collaborator still has their cursor in" ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md)). "The agent sees who wrote each message and comment" ([Work in a shared thread](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#work-in-a-shared-thread)).
- **Sending supersedes.** "Sending another message during generation stops the current response and immediately sends the new message" ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md)). For an external harness: "Sending a prompt takes over any turn that's running in the thread, including a teammate's. Coordinate before you start work" ([Claude Code Plugin](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/claude-code#work-in-a-joined-conversation)).
- **Where the turn runs, and who pays.** Unchanged since the earlier note: on the sender's desktop, browser or runner, "never … on the thread owner's device or another participant's device", and model usage "belongs to whoever sends the request" ([Collaborate in a Thread](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#where-the-agent-runs)). Personal Runners add one more place: another of the sender's own computers, selected per thread, with "both devices … signed in to the same Delta account" ([Cloud Runners](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/cloud-runners#run-turns-on-your-own-computer-from-another-device)).

### 2.6 Comments

- **What the person sees.** Comment "the way you'd comment on a document: select a span of text in your own message or the agent's, and attach a note to it. The comment stays with the text it's about." Comments work on files and diffs too, where "comment mode is on by default, so typing creates a comment rather than editing the file". Comments have replies and inline images ([Comments](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/comments)).
- **When they reach the agent.** "Comments you write are pending until you next submit: they are delivered together with your message", or on their own. "The agent treats comments as feedback aimed at the exact passage it's attached to", and "its answer is linked back to your comment" ([How the agent responds](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/comments#how-the-agent-responds)). One comment chain can be sent to a new fork instead ([Send one comment chain to a fork](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/comments#send-one-comment-chain-to-a-fork)).
- **Underneath.** A comment is a delta ([Delta & Git](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/delta-and-git#what-is-a-delta)). The CLI's `comment` event carries the author, co-authors, whether an agent wrote it, the item or file it was delivered with, and a target: a transcript item and quote, or a file path with line regions "as they read when the comment was made" ([`thread-commands.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/thread-commands.ts#L78-L100)). Comments on deleted code reopen the file version they referred to ([Comments](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/comments#how-the-agent-responds)).

### 2.7 Forks, review threads and Land

- **Forks** open "as a subthread with a copy of the parent's conversation and Delta worktrees at the point you fork", then advance independently; changes come back only by asking an agent to merge them, and only within "the same thread family" ([Fork a thread](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/threads#fork-a-thread); [Merge](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/review-and-sync#merge-selected-worktrees-from-related-threads)).
- **Review threads.** A separate conversation attached to the work, whose agent writes a "change guide" first and "works in separate, forked Delta worktrees". **Submit Review** posts Approve or Request Changes to the parent as an avatar badge; **Pull Changes** applies "a snapshot of the review's edits" saved with the verdict. A review "follows its parent" for sharing ([Review in a review thread](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/review-and-sync#review-in-a-review-thread)).
- **Land** forks the thread into a Land subthread that runs a project's Land skill; "the button does not impose a Git workflow of its own", and the result shows as a card in both threads ([Land changes](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/review-and-sync#land-changes)). Pull requests are not replaced by a Delta mechanism; a PR opened from a shared thread can carry an "Open in Delta" link ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md)).
- **Agents message other threads** ("Sent Agent Message", "Received Agent Message"); "the receiving agent handles it during its current response or starts a new response if idle" ([Threads](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/threads#send-information-to-another-agent)).

### 2.8 Mentions and notifications

Typing `@` and choosing a person notifies them in their Inbox "as soon as you choose them, **before** you send", and "mentioning someone can also share a private thread with them". Someone with the thread open gets no separate notification ([Mention someone](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#mention-someone)). "Mentions written by an agent do not notify anyone" ([Delta 101](https://delta.dev/docs/delta-101.md)). The browser has the same Inbox and optional desktop notifications "while Delta Web is open" ([Delta on the Web](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/delta-on-the-web#inbox-and-notifications)). Mentions shipped in [0.19.0](https://delta.dev/docs/whats-in-the-latest#0.19.0); the web Inbox in [0.20.0](https://delta.dev/docs/whats-in-the-latest#0.20.0).

### 2.9 Web and mobile

Delta Web at [delta.dev/threads](https://delta.dev/threads) lists a person's and their organization's threads, opens shared links (on phones directly), and can be added to the Home Screen; it "still need[s] an internet connection" ([Delta on the Web](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/delta-on-the-web#install-delta-web)). On a phone, conversation, Changes, Files and open files share one tab bar, with reviews, Land and search under **More** ([Work on a phone](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/delta-on-the-web#work-on-a-phone)). A browser turn "works with replicated thread and project data. It has no shell or checkout"; for local tools the browser sends the turn to a Personal Runner, or to a Cloud Runner in the private beta ([Choose where to run a request](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/delta-on-the-web#choose-where-to-run-a-request)). Creating top-level threads, local projects and Land need the desktop app.

### 2.10 Offline

- **Local first.** Changes apply locally and sync when a connection returns, which "makes it straightforward to implement the entire spectrum of collaboration, from handing off a thread asynchronously to collaborating in real time" ([engineering post](https://delta.dev/blog/fine-grained-version-control-for-agents#architecture)).
- **But launch is online.** "Delta checks in with Zed's servers at launch: you need to be signed in, have accepted the Early Access Agreement, and be online for Delta to open a workspace" ([Troubleshooting](https://delta.dev/docs/troubleshooting#delta-wont-open-a-workspace)).
- **Conflicts.** When the server refuses local changes, the thread shows "This thread has diverged from the server", with **Keep Local Changes in a New Thread** or **Discard Local Changes** ([Troubleshooting](https://delta.dev/docs/troubleshooting#a-thread-says-it-has-diverged-from-the-server)). External harnesses get the same choice as `reload_required` (3.8).

### 2.11 What removes the friction

| Friction elsewhere | What removes it in Delta | Underneath |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting a teammate your uncommitted state | Nothing to commit, push or clone; the joiner's checkout appears | Worktree deltas over a per-thread sync object; checkouts built from a local clone or a fetch (2.1, 2.2) |
| Explaining what was tried | The thread is the handoff: conversation, tool calls, thinking and files together, linkable to a position | One record per thread (2.1, 2.4) |
| Turning feedback into a prompt | Comments and drafts go to the agent with the next send, each with its author | Comments and drafts as deltas; a send submits all trailing drafts and pending comments (2.5, 2.6) |
| Deciding who acts | Live drafts as chat, presence, following; a send takes over a running turn | Presence events; supersession (2.4, 2.5) |
| Different machines and tools | Each sender's turn runs where they are, on their own credentials; external harnesses join | Per-turn placement; the producer contract (2.5, section 3) |
| Worktree bookkeeping | Managed checkouts removed on archive and recreated from history | Worktree history kept in DeltaDB ([Delta Worktrees](https://delta.dev/docs/concepts/worktrees)) |
| Review and merge | Review threads with verdicts and Pull Changes; Land skills | Forked worktrees; merges within a thread family (2.7) |

The same documents state what the seamlessness doesn't cover: machine-local runtime state isn't synced, so "the resources available to the agent can therefore change between turns" ([Where the agent runs](https://delta.dev/docs/collaboration/collaborate-thread#where-the-agent-runs)); ignored files and local branches don't travel; a send can stop someone else's turn, and stopping "can't undo what tools already did" ([plugin guide](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#recover-safely)).

## 3. External harnesses: the plugin contract

### 3.1 Status

- **Documented, beta, versioned.** "Plugins use Delta CLI, which is in beta, so the commands and output your plugin relies on can change. The CLI reports which versions of that output it supports, so your plugin can stop and ask for an update instead of breaking." `delta --json thread contract` answers `{"operation":"contract","min":8,"max":8}`; "This page describes contract version 8, which Delta 0.22.0 introduced" ([Connect a session](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#connect-a-session)). The CLI guide adds: "Commands, flags, and output can change between releases" ([Get Started](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/get-started)).
- **Churn so far.** The plugin spoke contracts 6–7 at `500bde47` (2026-10-21) and speaks only 8 at `70aabf08`: "Neither the plugin nor the CLI has shipped, so nothing older is supported; raise this rather than keep a gate for an old CLI" ([`thread-commands.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/thread-commands.ts#L398-L408)).
- **Invited.** Delta asks for more harnesses ("we'd welcome help adding others"), names Pi as next, and calls the plugin "a reference implementation for connecting your preferred harness" ([blog](https://delta.dev/blog/claude-code-multiplayer#more-harnesses-to-come); [CLI page](https://delta.dev/cli)). "Use the CLI for everything. Don't read or write Delta's database directly" ([guide](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin)).
- **The CLI runs on the app.** "The installer downloads the latest stable Delta release, because the CLI runs on the Delta app … the CLI starts it in the background when a command needs it, and it exits when nothing needs it" (`~/Applications/Delta.app` on macOS, `~/.local/delta.app` on Linux). "The CLI needs Delta 0.22.0 or later" ([Install Delta CLI](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/get-started#install-delta-cli)). The plugin's fixtures name the native side's test modules (`delta_ipc::cli_output`), and its error codes include `app_unavailable` and `incompatible_app` ([fixtures README](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/testing/fixtures/cli/README.md?plain=1#L9-L18); [`error-codes.json`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/testing/fixtures/cli/error-codes.json#L39-L44)).

### 3.2 The verb surface

Scripts add `--json`; `thread list`, `search`, `show` and `status` print JSON anyway, `append` and `watch` use JSON Lines, and mounts print one JSON line when ready ([Use the CLI in scripts](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/get-started#use-the-cli-in-scripts); [guide](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin); the plugin's wrappers in [`delta-cli.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/delta-cli.ts#L225-L527)):

| Verb | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `thread contract` | The contract range; answers "from the CLI binary without starting delta-app" ([L45](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/delta-cli.ts#L45-L46)) |
| `thread create --source S --external-id E [--organization O --visibility private\|organization] [--parent P --name N --shared-worktrees\|--isolated-worktrees] [--prompt … --model …]` | Creates or finds the producer's thread; idempotent on the pair |
| `thread status T --source S --external-id E` | Access, visibility, upload state, attached worktrees and their checkouts, the producer's `next_sequence` and current turn |
| `thread share T --visibility organization`, `thread invite T EMAIL…` | Publish to the organization, or invite people (uploading privately first) |

### 3.3 Producer identity

"Each session needs two IDs: `--source`, a fixed name for your plugin, and `--external-id`, your agent's ID for the session. Delta uses the pair to find the session's thread", and "running `thread create` again with the same two IDs returns the same thread" ([Connect a session](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#connect-a-session)). `--producer-name` "is the name Delta shows for your agent's output" ([Send conversation updates](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#send-conversation-updates)). The plugin passes it on every append "so attribution never depends on a live watch", and keeps identity in the pair: the name "is only presentation" ([`delta-cli.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/delta-cli.ts#L384-L389); [`thread-commands.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/thread-commands.ts#L409-L414)). Status reports the actor the device records the producer as (`actor_id`), which items' authors name ([`thread-commands.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/thread-commands.ts#L26-L30)). A running-state record may carry `_meta.delta.producer_version` and `execution_location` ([`delivery.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/recording/delivery.ts#L373-L380)).

### 3.4 What an append carries

- **Records.** "Each record has `schemaVersion`, `sequence`, an optional `turnSequence`, and an ACP v2 `update`. Appending records activity. It doesn't run messages or tool calls." Supported updates: `user_message`, `agent_message`, `agent_thought`, `tool_call_update`, `state_update`, and `session_info_update` for the title. "Streaming chunk updates are rejected. To stream a message, send it again with the same `messageId` and its full content so far" ([Send conversation updates](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#send-conversation-updates)).
- **That is ACP's v2 draft.** Whole-message updates "for `user_message`, `agent_message`, and `agent_thought` … are upserts keyed by `messageId`"; v2 replaces v1's `oldText`/`newText` diff with "optional Git `--patch` text (`git_patch`) plus structured file operations"; `state_update` reports `running`, `idle` and `requires_action` ([v2 overview](https://github.com/agentclientprotocol/agent-client-protocol/blob/1c34114c2f406d0739e7e36a5a99bb6059ee7caf/docs/rfds/v2/overview.mdx?plain=1#L62-L68)). The plugin builds exactly these from Claude Code's messages, with edits as `git_patch` diffs, and borrows tool titles from `claude-agent-acp` "so a recorded call reads as it does when Delta runs Claude itself" ([`content.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/recording/content.ts#L188-L194), [L272-L333](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/recording/content.ts#L272-L333)).
- **Turns.** "Start it with a `running` state update whose `turnSequence` equals its own `sequence`. Give the turn's content and its final `idle` update the same `turnSequence`." "A `state_update` without `turnSequence` is recorded as history" ([guide](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#send-conversation-updates)).
- **Receipts.** One per record: `{"operation":"appended","thread_id","sequence","next_sequence","duplicate","disposition":"recorded"|"fenced"|"duplicate","turn_sequence","version"}` ([fixture](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/testing/fixtures/cli/thread-append.batch.jsonl#L1-L3)). "`fenced` means the record belongs to a turn that someone else took over, and nothing was written" ([Recover safely](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#recover-safely)).
- **Subagents** are child threads (`--parent`), with shared, isolated or no worktrees; a prompt from the parent agent names it in `_meta.delta.sender.external_id`. "Recording a child doesn't start a Delta turn. Your agent still runs the subagent" ([Record subagents](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#record-subagents)).

### 3.5 What a watch emits

"The stream starts with `ready`, then the thread's current items, then `live`, then changes as they happen." Event types: `ready`, `item` (replaces an item), `item.append`, `remove`, `live`, `turn` (`idle` or `running`, with the user who started it), `producer.turn` ("your turn's state, including supersession"), `presence`, `children`, `child`, `child.remove`, `worktrees`, `comment`, `comment.remove`, and a terminal error envelope. Passing the producer pair hides the producer's own items; `--participate` "shows the user to collaborators as taking part through your agent … for as long as the watch runs". "If your agent's process exits while its turn is still running, Delta ends the turn when the watch ends"; the watch takes its parent process as the agent unless `--owner-pid` names another. "Each `thread watch` starts with a fresh snapshot, and there is no way to resume a previous stream" ([Receive shared activity](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#receive-shared-activity); [Recover safely](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#recover-safely); types in [`thread-commands.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/cli/thread-commands.ts#L51-L118)). An item has an id, a kind, `after_id` for ordering, a turn id, an author, `open` while streaming, and kind-specific content.

The guide's instruction for inbound items: "Add remote activity to your agent's context without automatically starting a turn."

### 3.6 Checkouts: the sharer's and the joiner's

- **Sharer.** `worktree create "$PWD"`, `worktree attach REF --thread T`, then `worktree mount REF "$PWD" --existing-checkout`, which "uploads the checkout and keeps syncing it for as long as the command runs"; stopping it with SIGTERM uploads what's pending, then stops syncing. "A mount whose process is gone doesn't keep Delta running" ([Connect a session](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#connect-a-session)). Sync runs "in both directions" ([Work with worktrees](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/get-started#work-with-worktrees)). The plugin attaches the Git top level of Claude's directory ([`share.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/sharing/share.ts#L68-L70)).
- **Joiner.** `thread mount LINK --owner O` prints one line with the thread's `directory`, "a folder containing a link to each worktree's checkout", and keeps running; the agent moves into that directory. When the mount stops, Delta uploads pending changes, "then deletes the checkouts it created"; "Ignored files and local Git state, such as branches, aren't shared or kept". A `worktrees` event means re-mounting under the same owner ([Prepare files for joining](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#prepare-files-for-joining)). A joiner should "record only new activity from then on. Don't export their previous session".

### 3.7 Turn ownership

Delta keeps one running turn per thread. A producer starts one with its `running` record, which takes over any running turn; when someone else takes over, the producer sees `producer.turn` `superseded` or a `fenced` receipt and must "stop the local turn … Keep late output's original `turnSequence`. Stopping can't undo what tools already did" ([Recover safely](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#recover-safely)). The plugin aborts the Claude turn and tells the person "Already performed tool effects are not rolled back" ([`delivery.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/recording/delivery.ts#L311-L317), [L493-L511](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/recording/delivery.ts#L493-L511)).

### 3.8 Recovery, offline and versions

"Save records before sending them. Number them consecutively from 1, and never reuse a sequence number for different content"; resend unchanged on failure; expect partial batches; retry only when `error.retryable` is true; "If you lose your saved records or sequence numbers, you can't restart the same session from sequence 1. Start a new session ID instead, which creates a new thread" ([Recover safely](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#recover-safely)). Offline: `thread create --organization … --visibility private` returns at once and uploads in the background, and `append --acknowledge local` acknowledges on the device; a server refusal surfaces as `reload_required` until the person forks or discards ([Record while offline](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#record-while-offline)). The plugin records offline only for its own threads: "A joined session writes into someone else's thread, so it keeps server-confirmed delivery and holds Claude while Delta is unreachable" ([`delivery.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/recording/delivery.ts#L46-L55)).

### 3.9 How the Claude Code plugin uses it

- **Who said what, for the agent.** Teammates' messages and comments are added to Claude's conversation as user rows such as "[Delta] Ada, message (item …):" ([`conversation.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/watch/conversation.ts#L264-L269)), through Claude Code's plugin engine (`session.append`), and held while Claude's own turn runs "so it never lands inside one" ([`rows.tsx`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/watch/rows.tsx#L32-L60)). For the person: "Messages from teammates appear in your transcript but don't start a Claude turn. Claude sees them with your next prompt." "Another agent's thinking appears in your transcript, but Claude doesn't receive it" ([Work in a joined conversation](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/claude-code#work-in-a-joined-conversation); [`conversation.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/watch/conversation.ts#L5-L28)).
- **Joining a long thread.** The snapshot gives Claude the newest items up to 400,000 characters ("about 100k tokens"), with one row saying how to read older ones ([`conversation.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/watch/conversation.ts#L21-L28)). Sharing exports at most "the newest 4,096 messages of the current conversation" ([What sharing doesn't include](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/claude-code#what-sharing-doesnt-include)).
- **What sharing includes.** Messages and replies "including thinking", tool calls with inputs and outputs, images, and "your checkout's files, including staged, unstaged, and untracked changes"; not unsent prompts, ignored files, local branches, `--add-dir` folders or earlier sessions ([What sharing includes](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/claude-code#what-sharing-includes)).
- **Automatic syncing** per repository (`manual`, `private`, `organization` in `.delta/settings.json`, kept out of Git); "Claude keeps working offline" ([Set up automatic syncing](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/claude-code#set-up-automatic-syncing)).
- **Presence.** Arrivals count after 3 s and departures after a 5 s grace; "Presence lists people, not clients" ([`presence.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/watch/presence.ts#L9-L28)).
- **The plugin is a Claude Code mod**, registered as a module ([`hooks.json`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/hooks.json#L1-L4)), and it relies on Claude-specific capabilities the guide tells other harnesses to replace: "Claude's function hooks, `/clear`, `/cd`, trust prompt, and transcript APIs are host-specific" ([Adapt the reference](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/build-a-plugin#adapt-the-reference-to-your-host)).

### 3.10 What the plugin doesn't cover

- **SDK sessions.** "The plugin doesn't support Claude Code cloud sessions, print mode (`claude -p`), or SDK sessions yet" ([Requirements](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/claude-code#requirements)); in its code, "Print and SDK sessions keep no conversation a plugin can add rows to" ([`rows.tsx`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/watch/rows.tsx#L190-L194)). `claude-agent-acp`, which weave's Claude preset runs, is an "ACP adapter for the Claude Agent SDK" built on `@anthropic-ai/claude-agent-sdk` ([README](https://github.com/agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp/blob/2fd46c866d4ff2939fe1b1245b15a536d6de7c0b/README.md?plain=1#L1-L7); [`package.json`](https://github.com/agentclientprotocol/claude-agent-acp/blob/2fd46c866d4ff2939fe1b1245b15a536d6de7c0b/package.json#L69)).
- **Interactive only.** "Sharing and joining need an interactive Claude Code terminal or Desktop Code tab" ([README](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/README.md?plain=1#L132)).
- **Nothing of permissions.** The record kinds have no permission request, elicitation, plan, mode or terminal output (3.4), and Delta has no permission system (5.5). The plugin leaves out `ExitPlanMode` because "Delta has no plan mode" ([`content.ts`](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/hooks/recording/content.ts#L258-L264)).

## 5. Constraints

### 5.1 Pricing

The [pricing page](https://delta.dev/pricing) shows Zed's plans: Personal at $0, to "use your existing API keys or external agents with Delta", including "Code with teammates and agents", and Pro at $10 a month with "$5 of tokens included each month" for Zed-hosted models (vendor figures). "Delta uses the same account and plans as Zed." Nothing states what changes after the beta. Model usage is the sender's (2.5), and an external harness bills its own provider.

### 5.2 Openness and self-hosting

No Delta or DeltaDB source is public (see the snapshot paragraph). The 2025 plan to "open-source it, and offer an optional paid service" ([identity note §1.1](agent-identity-and-where-agents-run.md#11-what-the-zed-team-has-published)) is not repeated in any 2026 source read. The nearest thing to self-hosting: DeltaDB "runs on our multi-tenant deployment, as well as on single-tenant deployments for customers who want to 'bring their own Cloudflare'", and is "designed to be portable to other platforms, so long as they provide an object store, a fault-tolerant Postgres database for authentication, and a way to spin up addressable single-threaded processes" ([engineering post](https://delta.dev/blog/fine-grained-version-control-for-agents#one-durable-object-per-thread)). No documentation describes how to get a single-tenant deployment. Sign-in is a Zed account through GitHub OAuth, with the session in the operating system keychain ([Sign In](https://delta.dev/docs/account/authenticate)).

### 5.3 Data that leaves the machine

- **Stored on Delta's servers** (Cloudflare R2, Durable Objects, KV, D1): Git objects of attached repositories, the thread's deltas, the share record, the thread catalog, the remote URL, collaborators' GitHub logins, commit identities and messages, synced account settings including provider API keys, and Cloud Runner sandboxes ([Data Storage](https://delta.dev/docs/privacy-and-security/data-storage#what-delta-stores)). Deleting a thread locally "does not reliably remove copies on your other devices or delete the thread's history from Delta's servers"; contributions to shared threads "may be retained".
- **To model providers:** the conversation, "collaborator messages and comments", tool output and files read, and excerpts of past threads ([AI requests](https://delta.dev/docs/privacy-and-security/privacy#ai-requests)).
- **Telemetry and crashes.** Telemetry is tied to the account and "Delta does not currently provide a setting to disable telemetry"; crash reports go to Sentry and "can contain thread content" ([Telemetry](https://delta.dev/docs/privacy-and-security/privacy#telemetry)). The CLI sends the same kind of telemetry ([Get Started](https://delta.dev/docs/cli/get-started#install-delta-cli)), and the plugin reports which integration ran each command and when turns start and end, without messages, paths or error text ([README](https://github.com/zed-industries/delta-claude/blob/70aabf083dc985d231494d94ad45498bd9e6bc77/README.md?plain=1#L145-L152)).
- **Redaction.** Delta "discovers secret values locally and replaces them with `[REDACTED]` before thread content is stored, synchronized, or sent to a model provider", from environment variables, dotenv and mise files, home-folder credential files and known token formats; "Redaction is a safeguard, not a substitute" ([Security](https://delta.dev/docs/privacy-and-security/security#secret-redaction)). The docs don't say whether records appended through the CLI are redacted.

### 5.4 Terms

The [Early Access Agreement](https://delta.dev/early-access) (last updated 2026-08-05, before the CLI) governs the beta. It grants "a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, non-commercial, revocable, limited license … to use the Early Access Products to test their functionality and provide Feedback", and does not permit using them "for product development research or any commercial purpose". It forbids creating "any other product which is based on or derived from the Early Access Products or the Early Access Products' outputs" (§3), and treats "Your use of an Early Access Product" as Confidential Information, with no public announcements "without Zed's prior written approval" (§4). On third parties: "If Tester instead connects Tester's own API key, account, or external agent with a model provider or other third party … such use is governed by Tester's agreement with that third party". Liability is capped at $100, and on termination Zed may "withhold, remove or discard any Tester Data". The docs that invite third-party plugins (3.1) were published later; no source reconciles them with §3.

### 5.5 Agent permissions and sandboxing

Unchanged since 2026-10-22: "Delta does not have an agent permission system. Based on your instructions, the agent acts autonomously and does not ask before calling tools. This includes potentially destructive calls." "Delta does not sandbox agents." Delta loads a shared repository's configuration without review: `.agents/prepare` runs when Delta first mounts a managed checkout, `.envrc` runs through `direnv`, and rules files and skills "become instructions to the agent" ([Agentic Safety](https://delta.dev/docs/privacy-and-security/agentic-safety)). Threads isolate project files but not the machine ([Threads](https://delta.dev/docs/agents/threads#thread-lifecycle)).

## 6. Open questions

- **The Early Access Agreement and plugins.** Whether a third-party harness plugin, such as a weave bridge, counts as a "Derivative Work" or as "product development research" under §3, given that Delta's own docs ask for plugins. Not answered by any source; a question for Zed.
- **Redaction of appended records.** Whether `thread append` input passes through the same secret redaction as the app's own threads (5.3).
- **The Delta app on a headless Linux Host.** The CLI starts the desktop app in the background and keeps its session in the system keychain; 0.22.0 fixed a crash "if a Linux system-keyring prompt ends unexpectedly" ([release notes](https://delta.dev/docs/whats-in-the-latest#0.22.0)). Whether this works on a server with no display or keyring, such as a weave Host on Linux (WVE-116, WVE-119), is not documented.
- **`requires_action` and stop reasons.** Whether Delta accepts ACP v2's `requires_action` state or a `stopReason` on the idle update. The guide names only `running` and `idle` (3.4).
- **The `delta` CLI's own help.** Flags beyond the guides (`thread watch --owner-pid`, `worktree merge`) were read only as the guides and plugin use them.
- **Contract stability.** How often the contract will move while the CLI is in beta. The only evidence is one move, 6–7 to 8, in a week (3.1).
- **What ACP support will be.** Whether Delta will launch ACP agents itself, as Zed's External Agents do, or accept ACP from outside. The roadmap's one sentence doesn't say.
- **Single-tenant DeltaDB.** Who can get a "bring your own Cloudflare" deployment, and at what price.
- **Pricing after the beta.** Not stated.

## 7. Fit for weave

### 7.1 Where weave stands

A Host runs one person's ACP agents; clients (the TUI, the CLI, the iPad and iPhone app) speak the Host protocol, and a thread's journal carries ACP v1's content unchanged ([spec](../specs/wve-85-host-protocol.md#L71-L82)). Since WVE-108 a script can follow an open thread without holding it (`session/attach` with `observe`) and watch every thread's listing (`sessions/watch`), and the CLI prints both as the protocol's own JSON ([spec](../specs/wve-85-host-protocol.md#L412-L427), [L459-L464](../specs/wve-85-host-protocol.md#L459-L464); [`cli/AGENTS.md`](../../cli/AGENTS.md#L37-L55)). Each turn's origin names a client and its device, a headless run, or another thread ([`sessions.rs`](../../protocol/src/sessions.rs#L99-L124)). Only people answer pending requests ([ADR 0020](../adr/0020-agents-reach-the-host-as-clients-do-and-act-only-on-threads-they-start.md#L19)). Threads run in Git worktrees that belong to the person, in worktrunk's layout, which the Host never removes on its own ([ADR 0019](../adr/0019-the-host-manages-worktrees-with-git-in-worktrunks-layout.md#L8-L10)). Hosts are peers with no coordinator, and services connect as clients ([ADR 0017](../adr/0017-hosts-pair-as-peers-through-the-tailnet-and-are-managed-through-their-cli.md#L8-L10), [L27](../adr/0017-hosts-pair-as-peers-through-the-tailnet-and-are-managed-through-their-cli.md#L27)). Agents sign in with their own CLIs, and weave "never reads or stores" their credentials ([`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md#L161-L162)). Since 2026-10-22, weave stays single-user: one Host serves one person, and sharing would happen between Hosts, never through tenancy inside one ([identity note §10.8](agent-identity-and-where-agents-run.md#108-sharing-deferred)).

Delta's collaboration rests on two layers: a replicated record per thread that every participant holds (2.1, 2.2), and, on top, conversation features (drafts, comments, mentions, review). Its external-harness contract lets a harness be one participant in that record while running its own agent on its own machine and login (section 3). That second shape is the one weave could take.

### 7.2 weave against the contract

| weave | Delta, contract 8 | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thread id (the agent's ACP session id) | `--source weave --external-id <thread id>` | Clean. The id survives reopen and Host restarts. |
| Journal position `{journal, seq}` | `sequence`, consecutive from 1, saved before sending | The bridge keeps its own sequence. A closed thread opened again starts a new journal, whose start is the agent's `session/load` replay ([spec](../specs/wve-85-host-protocol.md#L447-L457)), which must not be appended a second time. |
| `turnStarted` | `state_update` `running` with `turnSequence = sequence`, then `user_message` | Clean. |
| `turnSteered` | `user_message` in the running turn | Clean; Delta's own app supersedes instead of steering (2.5). |
| `turnEnded` | `state_update` `idle` | Clean; whether the stop reason survives is open (section 6). |
| `agent_message_chunk`, `agent_thought_chunk`, `user_message_chunk` (v1) | `agent_message`, `agent_thought`, `user_message` (v2, whole, with `messageId`) | Chunks must be coalesced and given ids; the Host already coalesces the last message for `LatestTurn.message` ([spec](../specs/wve-85-host-protocol.md#L409-L410)). |
| `tool_call`, `tool_call_update`, v1 diffs | `tool_call_update`, `git_patch` diffs | Needs a v1-to-v2 translation, as the plugin writes one from Claude's own format. |
| `plan`, modes, config options, commands, usage | none | Dropped. |
| `terminalOutput`, `terminalExit` | none | Dropped, or folded into the tool call's output. |
| `renamed`, `session_info_update` | `session_info_update` | Clean. |
| Pending requests | none | Stay in weave, which is weave's rule anyway; in Delta the turn just looks running. |
| Subagent threads | child threads (`--parent`, shared worktrees) | Clean. |
| Delegated threads, `TurnOrigin::Session` | child or top-level threads; `_meta.delta.sender` | Partial: Delta merges and reports only within a thread family. |
| `TurnOrigin::Client` (name, device), `Run` | one producer per thread, one display name | The detail is lost unless put in `_meta`. |
| The queue | none; drafts, and sends that supersede | No counterpart. Only a turn that starts is appended. |
| The thread's worktree | `worktree create` + `attach` + `worktree mount --existing-checkout`, for the mount process's life | Clean, and Delta can already adopt linked Git worktrees (2.2). But the person's worktree then syncs both ways: a teammate's edits land in it live. |
| Joining: a thread's directory is fixed when it starts | `thread mount LINK --owner O` gives a directory to move into | A new weave thread started in the mount's directory, with the mount process kept as long as the thread is open. Delta deletes those checkouts on unmount; they are Delta's, not ADR 0019 worktrees. |
| Remote items and comments | must reach the agent "without automatically starting a turn" | **No counterpart.** ACP can't add context without a prompt, and no client request puts anything before the agent except a prompt or a steer. |
| `producer.turn` `superseded`, `fenced` | the bridge sends `turn/cancel` | Works, but lets anyone in the Delta thread stop the person's running turn. |
| Clients attached | `--participate` presence | The bridge's watch shows the person present "through weave". |

### 7.3 Option (a): weave as a producer in Delta threads

**Shape.** A client of the Host, as ADR 0017 shapes services and as the A2A note proposed for an A2A bridge ([§8.2](a2a-and-agents-controlling-threads.md#82-option-a-the-host-serves-a2a)): a `weave delta` command (or its own crate) on the Host's computer, which needs the Delta app installed there. Per bridged thread it would run `thread contract` once; `thread create`; `worktree create`, `attach` and `mount --existing-checkout` for the thread's worktree; follow the thread with `observe` from a saved position whenever `sessions/watch` shows it open (turns run only in open threads), translate (7.2) and append from a durable outbox; and run `thread watch --participate`, cancelling the turn on supersession. `weave delta share|invite|join` would wrap `thread share`, `invite` and `mount`. It can be built from what WVE-108 added, with one exception.

**The exception is inbound.** Delta's seamlessness for the agent is "Claude sees them with your next prompt" (3.9). weave has nowhere to put a teammate's message or comment between turns: queuing it would start a turn on the person's login, against Delta's guidance; steering only works while a turn runs. It needs a Host addition: context held for a thread's next turn, journaled so every client sees it, and prepended to whatever prompt starts the next turn, from any client, the queue or a run. Without it the bridge is outbound only: teammates watch, comment and review the weave thread in Delta, and the person reads their replies in Delta or has the agent read them with `delta thread show`.

**Costs.** The v1-to-v2 translation and the outbox with receipts, mount supervision, a watch that rebuilds from a snapshot on every reconnect, the Host addition, and keeping all of it in step with a contract that is a week old, in beta, and moved once already (3.1). The watch's turn-ending behaviour follows its owner process (3.5), and the Host protocol doesn't tell clients an agent's process id. The Delta app must run on each Host's computer, which is open for a headless Linux Host (section 6).

**Risks.** Everything a shared thread contains leaves the machine, thinking and every non-ignored file included, and sharing grants repository-wide history (5.3, 2.3). Telemetry can't be turned off. A teammate's edits land in the person's worktree while it is mounted, under the agent and the person's terminals. A teammate can stop the person's running turn (3.7). Joined checkouts run `.agents/prepare` from someone else's repository (5.5). The Early Access Agreement's §3 may not allow it (5.4).

**What it keeps.** The person's agent still runs in weave, under weave's permission policy, on the person's login, with pending requests answered by the person in weave's clients. Delta's missing permission system then applies only to Delta's own agents on other people's machines.

**What the shared thread becomes.** Several agents take turns in it: a teammate's send runs Delta's own agent, or their own Claude Code, on their machine (2.5), and the person's weave agent sees that agent's messages, not its thinking, at its next prompt (3.9). The conversation is shared; no agent's session is.

### 7.4 Option (b): ACP, once Delta ships it

If Delta's ACP support works as Zed's External Agents do, Delta would launch an ACP agent such as `claude-agent-acp` itself, on the sender's machine, inside a Delta thread. weave would take no part: its Host isn't an ACP agent and doesn't aim to serve ACP clients ([ADR 0008](../adr/0008-weave-daemon-is-the-host.md#L8)). Presenting a weave thread to Delta as an ACP agent would bring back the ACP face ADR 0008 removed, and Delta would place collaborators' turns on their own machines anyway (2.5), so they wouldn't reach the person's Host. This option is a way for the person to use Delta, not a way to collaborate from weave. It needs no weave work, and nothing about its timing or form is known (section 4).

**The shortcut doesn't work either.** Installing `delta-claude` into the Claude Code that weave runs won't record weave's Claude threads: `claude-agent-acp` runs Claude through the Agent SDK, and the plugin doesn't support SDK sessions (3.10). Codex threads have no plugin at all.

### 7.5 Option (c): borrow ideas, within one person and peer Hosts

Delta's conversation features don't depend on its replicated store in the parts worth having for one person with several clients and Hosts. Candidates, in order of value:

1. **Context held for the next turn.** The Host addition 7.3 needs, worth having alone: notes a person leaves from any client, say on the iPad while reading a long answer, that every client shows and that go with the next prompt, as Delta's pending comments do (2.6). It sits beside the queue: queued prompts start turns, held context waits for one. Medium: a journaled list per thread, like the queue, and client support.
2. **Comments anchored to a passage.** On top of 1: a comment that quotes a transcript passage or diff lines and names its journal position or file and lines, delivered as structured context so the agent answers it point by point ("feedback aimed at the exact passage", 2.6). Mostly in clients; the anchor is a journal position, which weave already has.
3. **Links to a position.** A link naming the Host, the thread and a journal position that opens any client there, as Delta's position links do (2.4). It helps one person move between the TUI and the app, and between peer Hosts. Small.
4. **Drafts kept by the Host.** Delta's drafts "stay if you close and reopen the thread" and appear on every device (2.5). A per-thread draft on the Host would let the person start a prompt on the phone and finish it at the desk. Small to medium; worth it only if the person misses it.

Already within reach, with no change: review as a delegated thread in its own worktree whose verdict comes back as a report ([ADR 0023](../adr/0023-agents-act-on-any-thread-and-the-hosts-reports-tell-nobody-back.md#L8)), and a Land-style skill in the repository.

Not worth borrowing: sending to supersede a running turn (weave's queue and steer are the deliberate alternative, [spec](../specs/wve-85-host-protocol.md#L650-L710)); presence and following, which serve several people; per-sender billing; and a replicated store of weave's own, which is the multiplayer substrate the single-user decision defers.

### 7.6 The single-user decision, and sharing between Hosts

- **Fits inside the decision.** Option (a), because Delta carries the people: the person's weave thread is one participant, other people never connect to the Host, and each person's turns run on their own login. It also sidesteps the identity note's open question on Anthropic's terms for shared use ([§9](agent-identity-and-where-agents-run.md#9-open-questions)): no one else's prompt starts a turn on the person's subscription; teammates' text reaches the agent only inside a prompt the person sends. Option (c) is single-user throughout.
- **Would need the decision revisited.** Anything that lets other people attach to the person's Host or prompt its threads; anything that gives weave per-person attribution inside a thread beyond text in a prompt; a weave-native shared record across different people's Hosts.
- **Does any current design close off sharing between Hosts later?** No. Delta shows what sharing in its shape needs: a record above each harness's own session that several sessions produce into, stable ids for items, attribution per turn, and files for each participant. weave's journal can feed such a record, as the bridge would; its thread id serves as an external id; `TurnOrigin` can gain a person later as an addition; and Git worktrees can be adopted or mounted, as Delta adopts linked Git worktrees. ADR 0017's "no coordinating Host" means the record would live in a service, which the ADR allows, and Delta reached through a bridge is one such service. One weakness worth knowing: items have no ids that survive a closed thread being opened again, since the new journal starts with the agent's replay at new positions. A sharing layer has to recognise that replay, which costs nothing to note now and would be awkward to discover later. Named turns, which the A2A note recommends ([§8.4](a2a-and-agents-controlling-threads.md#84-option-c-borrow-ideas-recommended)), would give a bridge a stable key per turn, though not per item.

### 7.7 Recommendation

**Don't integrate yet. Prepare for option (a), skip option (b), and take up idea 1 of option (c) only on its own merits.**

- **Option (a) is the right shape, and it fits the single-user decision.** It is what Delta built its CLI for, and it gives the person Delta's collaboration (teammates in Delta's app, browser or Claude Code; shared files without push and pull; comments and review) while their own work stays in weave. But three things argue against building it this month: the contract is a week old, in beta, and has already moved; the Early Access Agreement as written forbids derivative products and "product development research", and nothing reconciles that with the plugin guide; and its inbound half needs a Host addition first.
- **Before building it:** ask Zed whether a weave plugin is welcome under the agreement, and wait for a sign that the contract holds, such as the CLI leaving beta, contract 8 lasting through a few releases, or the Pi plugin shipping on it. If a real collaborator is waiting, an outbound-only spike outside the workspace (`weave threads follow --json`, a translator, `delta thread append`) would answer the open questions on the Linux Host, redaction and how a weave thread renders in Delta, without committing weave to anything.
- **Option (b)** needs no weave work and gives weave nothing. Installing `delta-claude` into weave's Claude doesn't work.
- **Option (c):** context held for the next turn, with anchored comments on top, is the one idea worth an issue, and only if the person finds themselves wanting to annotate before prompting. It is also the piece option (a) is missing, so building it first doesn't waste work if the bridge comes later. Position links are small and can come with it.
